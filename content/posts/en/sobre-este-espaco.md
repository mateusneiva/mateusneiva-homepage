## From projects to stories

A project is more than its final result. It's also the sum of the decisions, experiments, and details discovered along the way. This space brings together both sides: what's being built and what can be learned while building it.

The portfolio now highlights four projects with different goals:

- **[Licht Launcher](https://github.com/licht-launcher/licht-launcher)**: an unofficial Minecraft Java launcher for Windows and Linux, built with Tauri, Rust, and React.
- **[Softness](https://github.com/mateusneiva/softness)**: a streetwear e-commerce experience, from storefront to checkout.
- **[Polaris Kit](https://github.com/polaris-kit/polaris-kit)**: React components, accessibility, design tokens, and theming.
- **[Shiva Toolbox](https://github.com/shiva-toolbox/shiva-toolbox)**: modular automation for Discord communities.

Each project has its own page. Licht Launcher, for example, got a longer write-up on its architecture, challenges, and decisions, with room for screenshots of the app throughout.

## A place for notes

The posts section is designed for writing about development, technical decisions, and discoveries. Every article is published here, with room to give ideas context and keep the focus on reading.

Behind-the-scenes notes belong here too: why I chose a path, what went wrong, and what I learned from it. Short pieces when that is enough, longer ones when the subject asks for it.

The idea is simple: give code some context and keep what is worth sharing.
